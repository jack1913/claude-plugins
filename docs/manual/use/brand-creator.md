# <picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="../../assets/brand/brand-creator-dark.svg"><img src="../../assets/brand/brand-creator.svg" alt="brand-creator mark" width="32" height="32"></picture> brand-creator

Make a logo, colours and every brand file your product needs, step by step, with you deciding at each step.

```text illustrative
/plugin install brand-creator@jack1913
```

It needs a one-time `/brand-creator:setup` after you install; the [install steps](../../../plugins/brand-creator/README.md#install)
say what it downloads.

- [Make your first brand](../../../plugins/brand-creator/docs/manual/getting-started/make-your-first-brand.md): say what you need and pick from three directions.
- [Make icons and images from your logo](../../../plugins/brand-creator/docs/manual/use/make-icons-and-images.md): favicons, app icons and social images, no project needed.
- [Name your brand](../../../plugins/brand-creator/docs/manual/use/name-your-brand.md): get name ideas, or check a name.
- [Make the brand book](../../../plugins/brand-creator/docs/manual/use/make-the-brand-book.md): one page of brand rules.
- [Take your brand to the web](../../../plugins/brand-creator/docs/manual/getting-started/take-your-brand-to-the-web.md): sample pages for your site, a check of any page against your brand, and a brand kit for your product, step by step.
- [Check a page against your brand](../../../plugins/brand-creator/docs/manual/use/check-a-page.md): see where a page breaks your colours, fonts, logo or facts.
- [What it leaves to you](../../../plugins/brand-creator/docs/manual/reference/what-it-never-does.md): the steps that stay yours.

Everything else is in the [brand-creator manual](../../../plugins/brand-creator/docs/manual/README.md).
