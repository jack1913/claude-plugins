# puppet-master

Coordinate your Claude sessions from one session: hand each job to an open session or a background worker.

```text illustrative
/plugin install puppet-master@jack1913
```

- [Run background workers](../../../plugins/puppet-master/docs/manual/use/run-background-workers.md): hand jobs to your open sessions from one session, one job each, and get the analyst's report at the end of the day.

Everything else is in the [puppet-master manual](../../../plugins/puppet-master/docs/manual/README.md).
