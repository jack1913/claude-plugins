# Acme checklist

Go through it before you hand anything over. A "no" is fixed, or shown to the owner; never waved through. Generated from `rules.json`: never edit it here.

## Logo

- [ ] The logo comes from its files in `brand/assets/logo/`, used as they are.
- [ ] Up to 32 px the small mark uses `brand/assets/logo/grid-mark.svg`.
- [ ] At larger sizes the large mark uses `brand/assets/logo/mark.svg`.
- [ ] At 32 px and below the accent holds a solid 4 x 4 px square.

## Colour

- [ ] Every colour is a token role from `brand/assets/tokens/tokens.css`, in both themes where the material has them.
- [ ] Every ui-text pair reaches at least 4.5:1 (`brand.md` lists the pairs and their ratios).
- [ ] Every ui-text-large pair reaches at least 3:1 (`brand.md` lists the pairs and their ratios).
- [ ] Every ui-component pair reaches at least 3:1 (`brand.md` lists the pairs and their ratios).
- [ ] Every brand-rule pair reaches at least 3:1 (`brand.md` lists the pairs and their ratios).
- [ ] Every brand-rule pair reaches at least 2:1 (`brand.md` lists the pairs and their ratios).

## Claims

- [ ] Every number, feature, price, client and date is in `facts.json`; a missing one is a visible placeholder.
- [ ] Sample data is labelled as sample data.

## New things

- [ ] Anything the brand has not decided is labelled a proposal and shown to the owner.
- [ ] Every rule in `golden-rules.md` holds.
