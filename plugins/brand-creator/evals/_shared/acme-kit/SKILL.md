# Acme brand

Edition 1: approved on 2026-10-17. Written by brand-creator at the owner's explicit OK (gate G5); it is generated by brand-creator from the tokens, BRAND-STATE.md and FACTS.md; edit those, then regenerate. Do not edit it by hand.

Use this whenever you make or review material for Acme.

## How to read the kit

Every line in the kit has one of three weights.

| Weight | What it is | What you do with it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed | the identity: the logo files (and the signature move, when the brand has one), the token colours and their contrast, the type, the "never" list below, claims that are true, and each rule below that `golden-rules.md` does not sort as a default | keep it in every piece |
| Default | the brand's usual pattern and its practice: the other token values (spacing, radius, motion timing), the steps below and in the recipes, the checklist | use it, or do better; leaving it needs no permission, only a reason you can state |
| Example | one good application: a reference page, an approved ad or deck, a sample layout, sample copy | take what helps |

In the brand's own files the heading a line stands under (Fixed, Defaults, Examples) says its weight, and `golden-rules.md` sorts the brand's rules that way. The tag at the end of a line says where the line comes from: `[rule: <id>]` is a decision the brand recorded, `[example]` is an illustration. Where no heading says the weight, the tag decides: a line tagged `[rule: <id>]` is fixed, numbered or not; a choice made for a reference page (`[rule: site:<id>]`) is a default; a line tagged `[example]` is an example. Numbered steps with no tag are practice.

Three things change the brand for everyone, so they are never made up: a new brand colour, a claim that `facts.json` does not hold, and a redrawn logo. When the work needs one, finish without it, or with a visible placeholder marked as a sample, and tell whoever maintains the brand what was missing. Anything else the kit does not cover is yours to decide, in line with the fixed parts: say what you decided. "The owner" in this kit is whoever maintains the brand.

A weak spot in the kit is never a reason to ship that weak spot. Where an example or a snippet breaks a fixed line, or you can simply do better, do better in your own copy.

## How to work

This is the brand's own practice. Follow it, or use a better way of your own: the fixed parts hold either way.

1. Name what you are making and the moment it is for (a page, a post, an ad, a deck; a launch, a reminder, an error).
2. Read `golden-rules.md`: what every piece keeps, and what it may change.
3. Open the recipe for that material in `recipes/`, one file per kind of material. When none fits, work from `brand.md` and say what no recipe covers.
4. Take colours, type, spacing and motion from the tokens (`brand/assets/tokens/tokens.css`). A new brand colour is never made up; a role the tokens lack (a state colour, a raised surface, a shadow) is built from the colours they have.
5. Take the logo from its files in `brand/assets/logo/` as they are: never redrawn, recoloured or typed out. `snippets/logo.html` places it in light and dark.
6. Take every claim (a number, a feature, a price, a client, a date) from `facts.json`. When one is missing, leave a visible placeholder marked as a sample, or leave the claim out, and tell whoever maintains the brand.
7. Anything else the brand has not decided (a layout, a use of colour, an animation, a line of copy) is yours to decide, in line with the fixed parts: say what you decided.
8. Before you hand anything over, go through `checklist.md`.

## The kit

Kit version 1.2 (kit.json).

This folder is a brand kit and this skill is its core. `kit.json` lists every file with its hash. Never edit a generated file here: change the brand project and run `brand-creator kit build --yes` again. Your own files (`EXTENDING.md` says which) are never overwritten by `kit build`; in a product repository, `install.py` updates one only while that repository has not changed it.

It works beside your web-design skill, never instead of it: load both when you build a page. A built site does not serve this folder, so copy the files you use from `brand/` into the product's own public or static folder and link them from there; `recipes/web-page.md` has the steps.

## Never

- Do not stretch, squash or rotate the mark.
- Do not recolour the mark outside the palette's roles, or put it on its own colour.
- Do not crowd the mark: keep its clear space free.
