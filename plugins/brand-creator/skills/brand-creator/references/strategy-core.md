Drafted by you from research and the brief, shown on the round 1 board (`data-section="strategy-core"`, the seven
rows of `templates/round-page.html`) for the owner's reaction. Never collected by interview, never left for the owner
to fill. Each field is one or two lines; facts come from FACTS.md only.

## The fields

| # | Field | What to write | Example (synthetic) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Category and alternatives | the category as buyers name it, and what they use instead, the status quo first | Hand tools for small workshops; instead: making do with old tools, big-box store brands, second-hand tools, borrowing |
| 2 | One target | one specific audience, in their own terms | Owners of one- or two-person workshops who repair before they replace |
| 3 | Positioning sentence | For <target> who <need>, <brand> is a <category> that <benefit>. Unlike <main alternative>, it <difference>. | For small-workshop owners who need tools that last, Acme makes small-batch hand tools that are repaired, not replaced. Unlike store brands, every tool has a spare-parts list. |
| 4 | Onliness line | <Brand> is the only <category> that <difference> for <target> in <place or moment> | Acme is the only toolmaker that publishes a spare-parts list for every tool it sells |
| 5 | Proof points (max 3) | facts from FACTS.md that back field 3; none invented | spare-parts list per tool; small batches; repair guide in the box |
| 6 | Not this / This | a pair of voice lines that shows the register (one pair per audience register when there are several) | Not this: "Revolutionary tools for the modern maker." This: "Fix it once. Then use it for years." |
| 7 | Guardrails | what the brand never says or shows, as the block below | no lifetime promises not in FACTS.md; no claims about competitors |

## How to draft it

| Field | Draft it from | Test before it goes on the board |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `research/research.md` "Competitive alternatives" (status quo first) | a real, named alternative, not "legacy solutions" |
| 2 | BRIEF.md Audience, sharpened by research | one person in one situation, not a demographic band |
| 3 | fields 1 and 2, the lineup and the map | the difference is provable from FACTS.md, and a competitor could not claim it within months |
| 4 | field 3 | put a lineup competitor's name in: if the line is still true, there is no difference yet; rewrite |
| 5 | FACTS.md rows only | each point has a row with a source; "Only you can confirm" items are not proof |
| 6 | the territory's register; existing copy | the "This" line could only be this brand's; the "Not this" line is a real category cliché |
| 7 | the Guardrails block below | every sample line on the board passes it |

In Quick (no P1 research) draft from the brief and your short category scan, and say on the board that the core rests
on a short scan. When a standalone brand speaks to two audiences (for example warm for private clients, strict for
boards), keep one mark, type and colour and give field 6 one pair per register; the board writes the same topic in
each register so the owner can compare.

## Guardrails block

Field 7 is a block with four lines, always present; write "none found" rather than dropping a line:

```
Regulatory: <rules from the regulated-industries table, each with its article and the date checked; UNVERIFIED rows marked>
Claims:     <what may be claimed only with a FACTS.md row: numbers, clients, awards, status words, comparisons>
Category:   <clichés from research/cliches.md the brand avoids unless a direction argues for one>
Family:     <the never-borrow list from BRIEF.md (both directions), or "standalone, none">
```

The block is copied into BRAND-STATE.md Notes after the owner reacts, and into every designer and copy prompt next to
FORBIDDEN. A board sample line that breaks a guardrail is a board defect, not a style choice.

## Notes

- The positioning-sentence and onliness forms follow widely used positioning practice; the exact wording in the
  original books is not quoted here (UNVERIFIED), so do not attribute quotes to them.
- Listing competitive alternatives first, with the status quo among them, follows a common positioning method whose
  original wording is likewise not quoted (UNVERIFIED).
- An archetype may appear as a one-line voice hint only, never as the strategy. If every direction shares one
  archetype, the directions are not divergent.
- The map axes on the same board come from the category's clichés (P1b), not from these fields.
- A regulated profession adds its rules to the Regulatory line (see [regulated-industries.md](regulated-industries.md)).
- After the owner reacts, carry the agreed core into BRAND-STATE.md Notes and into every designer prompt.
