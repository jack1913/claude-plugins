1Strategy core

Drafted from a short category scan and the brief. React to any line; nothing here is final.

Category and alternatives
Hand tools for small workshops. Instead people buy store brands, buy second-hand, or borrow.
One target
Owners of one- or two-person workshops who repair before they replace.
Positioning
For small-workshop owners who need tools that last, Acme makes small-batch hand tools that are repaired, not replaced. Unlike store brands, every tool has a spare-parts list.
Only we
Acme is the only toolmaker that publishes a spare-parts list for every tool it sells.
Proof points
Spare-parts list per tool; batches of 200; a repair guide in every box (FACTS.md rows 1-3).
Not this / This
Not this: "Revolutionary tools for the modern maker." This: "Fix it once. Then use it for years."
Guardrails
No lifetime promises; no claims about competitors; status words only from FACTS.md.

All three keep one solid shape and one accent dot, so they survive at 16 px. Measured on the logo sheet: the dot is 4 px wide at 16 px in all three; Keystone's wedge loses its taper below 20 px.

5Weak points

  • The three marks share one construction (shape plus dot); they differ in strategy, but a sharper owner may read them as one family.
  • Type is not chosen yet: every specimen uses the system sans, so voice differences rest on copy and colour.
  • Lantern's marigold is 3.1:1 on its ground: usable as a fill with an edge, never as text.

6None of these

If none of these is right, say so and what was missing. The next round starts from new territories and shows where this one sat on the map.

7My call

Recommendation: Compass: the steadiest answer to the brief, and it reads at 16 px.

Honest critique: Compass is the safest of the three; if you want to stand out in a loud category, Lantern is the braver pick and I would build it with the same care.
