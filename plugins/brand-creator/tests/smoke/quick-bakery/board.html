1Strategy core

Drafted from the brief. React to any line; nothing here is final.

Category and alternatives
A neighbourhood bakery. Instead people buy supermarket bread, or go to the chain café.
One target
Neighbours within a ten-minute walk who buy bread for the week on Saturday morning.
Positioning
For neighbours who want real bread close to home, Marlow Lane Bakery is a small bakery that sells what it baked that morning. Unlike the supermarket, it sells out and says so.
Only we
Marlow Lane Bakery is the only bakery on the lane that bakes sourdough every morning in the shop.
Proof points
Sourdough baked every morning; a queue on Saturdays; open six days (FACTS.md rows 1-3).
Not this / This
Not this: "Artisan excellence since forever." This: "Baked before you were up."
Guardrails
No health claims; no "best in town"; opening hours and prices only from FACTS.md.

5Weak points

  • Hearth is the closest to what a bakery would draw first; the glowing cut is its only argument.
  • Type is not chosen yet: every specimen uses a system face, so the voice differences rest on copy and colour.
  • None of the three has been seen on a paper bag or a shop sign at distance; that is the next check.

7None of these

If none of these is right, say so and what was missing. The next round starts from new territories and shows where this one sat on the map.

8My call

Recommendation: Hearth: it is the only direction that reads as warm at 16 px and on a paper bag, and its glowing cut is a shape nobody else on the lane has.

Honest critique: a loaf is the obvious bakery symbol; if the glow does not show on a printed bag, Queue is the braver choice.
