# Presentations

What a deck for this brand keeps, and how we usually make one. `SKILL.md` explains the three weights (Fixed, Default, Example). The brand's values live in the tokens and rules.json; a line you add that pins a value cites a token or a rule, or is tagged `[example]`.

## Fixed

Everything fixed in `SKILL.md` and `golden-rules.md` holds in a deck. So do the copy rules of the masters' README (`brand/assets/masters/README.md`), which the owner approved with the masters. Claims are fixed too: every number, feature, price, client and date a slide states is in `facts.json`, and a figure that is only an example is labelled as sample data.

## Defaults

### How we usually make one

This is the brand's own practice. Follow it, or use a better way of your own: the fixed lines hold either way.

1. Start from the approved deck: `brand/assets/masters/README.md` lists its files (HTML, PDF or PowerPoint) and the copy rules.

2. Keep each slide's layout. Take the slide type that fits what the slide says, and change only its copy and its data.

3. Take every claim (a number, a feature, a price, a client, a date) from `facts.json`. A figure that is only an example is labelled as sample data.

4. Nothing reaches outside the slide, and no text is covered by anything or runs into other text. A PowerPoint deck is checked in PowerPoint, never in Keynote, which ignores the fonts embedded in the file.

5. Anything new (a slide type, a layout, a chart, a line of copy) is yours to make in the same language, in line with the fixed parts: say what you decided.
