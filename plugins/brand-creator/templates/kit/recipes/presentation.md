# Presentations

Steps only. The brand's values live in the tokens and rules.json; a line you add that pins a value cites a token or a rule, or is tagged `[example]`.

1. Start from the approved deck: `brand/assets/masters/README.md` lists its files (HTML, PDF or PowerPoint) and the copy rules.

2. Keep each slide's layout. Take the slide type that fits what the slide says, and change only its copy and its data.

3. Take every claim (a number, a feature, a price, a client, a date) from `facts.json`. A figure that is only an example is labelled as sample data.

4. Nothing reaches outside the slide, and no text is covered by anything or runs into other text. A PowerPoint deck is checked in PowerPoint, never in Keynote, which ignores the fonts embedded in the file.

5. Anything new (a slide type, a layout, a chart, a line of copy) is a proposal: label it and ask the owner.
