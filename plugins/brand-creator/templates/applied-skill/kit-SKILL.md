## How to read the kit

Every line in the kit has one of three weights.

| Weight | What it is | What you do with it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed | the identity: the logo files, the token colours and their contrast, the type, the "never" list below, claims that are true, and each rule below that `golden-rules.md` does not sort as a default | keep it in every piece |
| Default | the brand's usual pattern and its practice: the steps below and in the recipes, the checklist | use it, or do better; leaving it needs no permission, only a reason you can state |
| Example | one good application: a reference page, an approved ad or deck, a sample layout, sample copy | take what helps |

In the brand's own files the heading a line stands under (Fixed, Defaults, Examples) says its weight, and `golden-rules.md` sorts the brand's rules that way. The tag at the end of a line says where the line comes from: `[rule: <id>]` is a decision the brand recorded, `[example]` is an illustration. Where no heading says the weight: a rule is fixed, a choice made for a reference page (`[rule: site:<id>]`) is a default, numbered steps are practice, and a line tagged `[example]` is an example.

Three things change the brand for everyone, so they are never made up: a new brand colour, a claim that `facts.json` does not hold, and a redrawn logo. When the work needs one, finish without it and tell whoever maintains the brand what was missing. Anything else the kit does not cover is yours to decide, in line with the fixed parts: say what you decided. "The owner" in this kit is whoever maintains the brand.

A weak spot in the kit is never a reason to ship that weak spot. Where an example or a snippet breaks a fixed line, or you can simply do better, do better in your own copy.
